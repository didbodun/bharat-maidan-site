Ukraine & India

A shared history, one milestone at a time. Explore key moments in relations between Ukraine and India, from independence to the present.

Explore the timeline ↓
1991→2026

Selected milestones · 1991–2026

  1. 1991

    Ukraine becomes independent

    Ukraine declared independence on 24 August. India recognised Ukraine as an independent country in December 1991.

  2. 1992

    Diplomatic relations begin

    On 17 January, India and Ukraine established diplomatic relations. India opened its embassy in Kyiv in May.

  3. 1993

    Ukraine opens its embassy in India

    Ukraine opened its embassy in New Delhi, creating a permanent diplomatic presence in India.

  4. 2012

    A presidential visit to India

    Ukrainian President Viktor Yanukovych paid a state visit to India in December. The visit included agreements on cooperation in several fields.

  5. 2022

    War and Operation Ganga

    Following Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine on 24 February, India launched Operation Ganga to evacuate Indian nationals, including many students, via neighbouring countries.

  6. 2024

    Prime Minister Modi visits Kyiv

    On 23 August, Narendra Modi visited Ukraine and met President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. It was the first visit by an Indian prime minister since Ukraine’s independence. Agreements covered agriculture, medical products, culture and humanitarian assistance.

  7. 2026

    The story continues

    This is the end of the exhibition’s time range, rather than a separate historical event. New milestones can be added as they are documented.
