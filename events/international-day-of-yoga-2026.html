12th International Day of Yoga in Kyiv

Sport | 21.06.2026

Introduction

The Embassy of India in Ukraine invites everyone to join the celebration of the 12th International Day of Yoga in Kyiv.

The event offers an opportunity to practise yoga together, enjoy an open-air community gathering, and celebrate the values of health, balance, and well-being.

Event details

  • Date: Sunday, June 21, 2026
  • Time: 9:00 AM
  • Venue: School No. 169, Volodymyra Salskoho Street, 2, Kyiv
  • Location note: The school is next to the Embassy of India in Ukraine.

What to expect

Based on last year’s format, the yoga session is expected to take place outdoors on the school stadium. Participants are therefore encouraged to come prepared for an open-air yoga practice.

  • A community yoga session
  • Outdoor practice at the school stadium
  • Free admission
  • Refreshments after the event

What to bring

Wear comfortable clothing suitable for yoga and outdoor movement. Bringing your own yoga mat and drinking water is also recommended.

Official announcement

For the original invitation and the latest updates, visit the Embassy’s Facebook post.

Conclusion

Participation is free. Join the 12th International Day of Yoga in Kyiv and begin your Sunday with movement, mindfulness, and community.
