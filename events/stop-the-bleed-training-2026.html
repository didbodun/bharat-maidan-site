Free Stop the Bleed Training

Education | 26.07.2026

Introduction

Registration is now open for a free Stop the Bleed training. Anyone may find themselves beside an injured person during the first critical minutes after trauma, and someone’s life may depend on the actions taken before medical professionals arrive.

Training details

  • Date: Sunday, July 26, 2026
  • Start time: 10:00 AM
  • Location: Registered participants will receive the venue by email one day before the training.
  • Participation: Free of charge

What you will learn

  • How to recognize life-threatening bleeding
  • How to use a tourniquet correctly
  • How to pack a wound
  • How to apply a pressure dressing
  • How to act quickly and confidently until medical professionals arrive

Certificate

Participants may request a personalized certificate of completion. The certificate processing fee is UAH 100.

Registration

Places are limited, so advance registration is required. Complete the registration form and join the training.

Conclusion

Learn practical bleeding-control skills that can help save a life during the crucial minutes before emergency medical assistance arrives.
